**Maker first comment**

Hi Product Hunt! I kept losing my train of thought between having a sentence ready and getting it into the text field I was using. So I made SayKuku around two gestures: Fn for Voice Input, Fn Fn for Voice Agent.

Voice Agent can rewrite selected text, translate, answer questions, and follow up. Memory is intentionally small: names, projects, and terms you add or confirm. The app currently needs macOS 15+ and your own Qwen API key; audio and permitted context are processed in your chosen region. History and Memory stay on your Mac as files without extra encryption.

If you try it, I’d especially like to know which Mac app you used and whether the text landed in the right place. I’ll be here answering questions.
